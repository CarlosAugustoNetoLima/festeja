What the website uses

This website does not use analytics tools or advertising cookies. For that reason there is no cookie banner: there is nothing for you to consent to.

In your browser, the website only stores two pieces of data, in sessionStorage (these are not cookies):

  • Basket (key festeja-cart-count): the number of tickets in your basket.
  • Purchases already recorded (key festeja-purchases): the order numbers of completed purchases in this session, so that the same purchase is not recorded twice.

This data stays in your browser only. It is not sent to the website or to third parties, and it is deleted when you close the tab.

Tickett ticket office

Purchases are made in the Tickett ticket office, in a window on this page, served through this website at /checkout. For the basket to work, we store in your browser the Tickett session cookie with the prefix tk_ (for example tk_PHPSESSID). It is strictly necessary for the purchase, is only used on /checkout addresses and is sent only to Tickett. Tickett has its own cookie and privacy policies. Please read them at checkout before you pay.

How to manage them

You can delete the website's data in your browser settings. Closing the tab deletes the basket and the purchases recorded in this session.
