Controller

The controller of personal data is RTP África, the organiser of Festival Festeja.

What data we collect

This website has no forms and no user accounts, and it does not use analytics tools. To browse it, we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal data.

  • In your browser, the website only stores the number of tickets in your basket and the purchases completed in this session (sessionStorage). See the cookie policy.
  • Buying tickets: this is done at Tickett's checkout, which collects the data needed for the purchase and has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can also lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

Contact

To exercise your rights, please write to us: contact to be confirmed.
