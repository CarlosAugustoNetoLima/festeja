Our commitment

Festival Festeja wants its website to be usable by everyone. Our aim is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Sufficient contrast between text, background and controls.
  • Full navigation using only a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus indicator.
  • Compatibility with screen readers: headings in order, links with clear names and text alternatives for images that carry content.
  • Respect for the reduced motion setting on your device.

Known limitations

  • The ticket office is a third-party window (Tickett). The accessibility of that part depends on the provider.
  • The artist photographs do not have audio descriptions.

Contact

If you come across a barrier on this website, please write to us: contact to be confirmed.
